# Funeral directors — target list (A1)

**Status: STARTER LIST, NOT VERIFIED.** Compiled 6 Sep 2026 from public web sources.
Every phone number and address below must be checked before anyone is contacted — directory
data goes stale, and branch addresses get conflated with head offices. Confidence is marked
per row. Treat this as a research head-start, not a call sheet.

**Before any cold call: screen against CTPS** (Corporate Telephone Preference Service). This is
a legal requirement for unsolicited B2B calls, not a courtesy.

---

## Who to exclude, and why

**Co-op Funeralcare and Dignity-owned brands buy print through national procurement.** A local
approach will not reach the buyer and cannot win on local service. Skip them entirely:

- **H. Copeland & Sons / Co-op Funeralcare** — 9 Bromley Road, Beckenham BR3 5NT
- **Francis Chappell & Sons** (Dignity) — 238 High Street, Beckenham BR3 1EN, plus Sydenham
- Any other Co-op Funeralcare, Dignity, or Funeral Partners branch

This is worth knowing before the list is worked: two of the closest funeral directors to the
Beckenham unit are both chain-owned, which may be part of why local trade work isn't already
flowing.

## Priority logic

**Multi-branch independents are the best targets.** They're independent, so they buy locally and
decide locally — but they have volume across several branches, so one relationship is worth
several. A single-branch independent is a smaller but easier win.

## Needs disambiguating before contact

- **Mears Family Funerals vs Steven Mears Funeral Directors** — both appear in Sydenham with
  separate websites (`mearsfamilyfunerals.co.uk`, `stevenmears.co.uk`). Possibly related firms,
  a split, or distinct businesses. Check before addressing either by the wrong name.
- **Grace Family Funerals / Graceful Funerals** (Lewisham) — two domain variants seen
