# Seasonal campaign calendar (A3)

**Status: ASSUMPTION, pending validation (C2).** This grid is derived from what's in the Bluwave
catalogue and how those products are bought — not from Bluwave's own sales data. Before spending
money on it, check calendar and planner revenue by month across two years in the Sage export. If
there's no Sept–Nov spike, rewrite this rather than defend it.

**The principle behind the timing:** for deadline-driven products the revenue doesn't defer, it
evaporates. Miss the calendar window and that money is gone for a year, not delayed. That's why
these campaigns are worth running *early*, and why they reach people before they start looking —
which is something analytics, seeing only people already searching, structurally cannot do.

## The grid

| Month | Push | Category | Why then |
|-------|------|----------|----------|
| **Jan** | New-year brand refresh — business cards, letterheads, comp slips | Business stationery | New year, new budgets; rebrands land in January |
| **Feb** | Spring event flyers; school prospectus planning | Flyers & leaflets, Schools | Lead time before Easter and open-day season |
| **Mar** | Financial year end — carbonless forms, updated stationery, annual reports | Copying & business forms, Booklets | Year end 31 Mar / 5 Apr; new details on stationery |
| **Apr** | New financial year stationery; AGM and annual reports | Business stationery, Booklets | Details change, reports fall due |
| **May** | Outdoor season — banners, site boards, hoarding, window graphics | Site & display boards, Marketing & promo | Construction and events season opens |
| **Jun** | Yearbooks; summer fair and sports day signage; prom invitations | Schools, Marketing & promo | Academic year ends — hard deadline |
| **Jul** | Yearbook final call; site boards for summer build programmes | Schools, Site & display boards | Last window before schools break |
| **Aug** | Quiet month — prep and pre-sell September | — | Use it to line up the autumn pushes |
| **Sep** | **Calendars & planners open. School open-day signage and prospectuses.** | Calendars, Planners, Schools | **The single most time-critical month** |
| **Oct** | **Calendars & planners peak.** Christmas cards. Retail packaging. | Calendars, Planners, Boxes & packaging | Must be delivered before January |
| **Nov** | Calendars & planners LAST CALL. Christmas packaging, swing tags, tissue paper | Calendars, Planners, Boxes & packaging | Final window before the year is lost |
| **Dec** | Last-minute retail packaging; pre-sell January stationery | Boxes & packaging | Quiet, but sets up Q1 |

## Non-seasonal

**Funeral stationery is demand-driven, not seasonal** — it arrives when it arrives and cannot be
campaigned for. Its growth comes from trade relationships with funeral directors (see
`03-trade-outreach-approach.md`), not from timing.

## Public sector overlay

Councils, NHS trusts and charities have their own rhythm worth layering on:

- **March** — financial year end; budget must be spent or lost. Historically a good month to ask.
- **April** — new budgets released.
- **Sept–Oct** — school open days and admissions; consultation and committee cycles resume.
- **May** — local election years bring notices, information leaflets and signage.

## What to do first

**The September push is the one that matters right now.** It's 6 September. Calendars and planners
should go to existing customers within the next week or two — they're the only item on this grid
whose window is currently open and closing.

**Do it as a test, not an act of faith.** Mail the existing customer base about calendars, count
what comes back, and that single result tells you whether seasonal campaigning works for this
business before anything larger is committed.

## Caveats

- The grid assumes Bluwave's customer mix resembles the client logos on the site (public sector,
  charities, schools, large-org branches). If the real revenue mix is different, the emphasis
  changes.
- Christmas cards and retail packaging appear here because the catalogue supports them
  (greeting cards, printed boxes, swing tags, tissue paper), not because there's evidence of
  demand. Lower confidence than the calendar and schools entries.
